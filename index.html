Quick answers

FAQ

Can I use the 3D skull asset in my own scene?

Totally—grab the downloadable GLTF from the spec sheet and credit Skelly Stickers in your project. The skull geometry is optimized for both AR filters and realtime apps.

Does the glow translate to real stickers?

Yes! Each sticker uses layered inks that soak up UV light. What you see in the viewer is modeled after the real phosphorescent gradients.

Will this run on low-power devices?

We detect prefers-reduced-motion, throttle animation, and gracefully fall back to a static render when WebGL is unavailable.
